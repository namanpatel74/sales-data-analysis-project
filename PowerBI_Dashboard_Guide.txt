WEEK 4 CAPSTONE – POWER BI DASHBOARD GUIDE

Import cleaned_sales_data.csv into Power BI Desktop.

Suggested dashboard:
1. KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Units Sold, Profit Margin.
2. Clustered bar chart: Sales by Country.
3. Bar chart: Sales by Product.
4. Column chart: Sales by Segment.
5. Line chart: Monthly Sales (Date hierarchy).
6. Slicers: Year, Country, Segment, Product, Discount Band.

Suggested DAX measures (table name: cleaned_sales_data):
Total Sales = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[ Sales])
Total Profit = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[Profit])
Total Units = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[Units Sold])
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
Average Sale = AVERAGE(cleaned_sales_data[ Sales])
Total Orders/Rows = COUNTROWS(cleaned_sales_data)
